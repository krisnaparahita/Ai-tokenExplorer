Words used on this page

Token
A small piece of a word. Roughly 3 tokens is 2 words. About 667 tokens fill a page and 200,000 fill a 300-page book (rules of thumb, not exact).
Fresh reading / reused from memory
Each step, the AI re-reads the whole conversation so far. Providers remember part of it ("caching"), which is usually cheaper and faster than reading it new.
Helper, hand-off, safety check
Modern AI tools often start other AI sessions to do part of the job. Those sessions use tokens too, so they are counted with the task that caused them.
Light · Typical · Heavy · Very heavy · Extreme
Size compared with your own typical task (the median): under half is Light, up to 2× Typical, up to 5× Heavy, up to 20× Very heavy, beyond that Extreme.
Simple · Multi-step · Team effort
How complicated the job was: Simple is a handful of steps; Team effort means several helpers or 150+ steps.
